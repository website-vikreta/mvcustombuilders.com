# Vision — MV Custom Builders

## Where We Are
A single coming-soon page: headline, short pitch, an email signup, real project photography
in the hero. No other routes exist yet. Everything past this point is future work, built
when there's real content (photography, project history, service specifics) to build it
from — not scaffolded ahead of time.

## The Story This Website Will Tell
A construction company whose actual work is renovating and restoring old and historic homes
— not a generalist contractor, not a new-build developer. The site's job is to make that
specialization obvious fast, and to make the visitor trust that their (irreplaceable, often
fragile) building is in careful hands.

**Arc:**
> This is trade work — real craft, real materials, real process.
> We restore what's worth keeping instead of tearing it out.
> Structurally sound. True to the era. Built to last.

## What "Done Well" Means Here
Not flashy. **Credible.** `docs/DESIGN_GUIDE.md` sets the bar: the site should feel like the
work looks — sturdy, honest, precise, lived-in. Not a slick tech startup, not a cheap
contractor flyer.
- Every claim has proof (a real photo, a specific detail) behind it
- Every photo is real work, not stock-photo cliché
- Mobile is the default experience, not an afterthought
- The one accent color (`--mvcb-orange`) is used like a spotlight, not a paint bucket

## What Success Looks Like
Not yet defined in measurable terms (traffic, lead volume, conversion targets) — confirm
with the client once the full site is being scoped, rather than assuming numbers. In the
near term, "success" for the current coming-soon page is: visitors understand what MV Custom
Builders does within seconds, trust the photography and copy, and sign up to be notified.
